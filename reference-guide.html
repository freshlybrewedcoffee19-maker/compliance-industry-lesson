What happens after a customer signs?

The provider helps establish a working security programme. The customer owns decisions and implementation. An appropriate independent assessor examines it when required.

  1. Confirm what the customer, bank or regulator requires.
  2. Agree the systems, people, services and data covered.
  3. Examine current practices and record gaps.
  4. Assign fixes, owners and dates.
  5. Implement and collect genuine evidence.
  6. Review readiness and undergo the applicable assessment.
  7. Keep checking, updating and improving after the assessment.

Can Perimattic stamp a PCI certificate?

A branded certificate alone is not recognised proof of PCI DSS compliance. PCI recognises its official forms, including the Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ), Report on Compliance (ROC), and Attestation of Compliance (AOC). The acquiring bank or payment brand determines the validation route.

We have not verified Perimattic’s authorisation as a Qualified Security Assessor (QSA) company. A claim of PCI-aware services or hiring a trained person does not establish company authorisation. Check the official company directory and the authorised markets.

A starting route is to provide readiness and agreed implementation work while an authorised QSA company performs the independent assessment where needed. Keep each party’s responsibilities and independence clear.

Becoming a QSA company is a separate programme involving company approval, experienced qualified employees, quality assurance, independence, insurance and continued qualification. Do not treat buying a training course as enough.

Which five should Perimattic prioritise?

No comparable India-wide popularity ranking was established. Laws, ISO certificates and SOC reports have different populations and counts. The following is a suggested starting order for software and IT-services clients, not a statistical ranking:

  1. ISO 27001 readiness: a broad security-management starting point.
  2. SOC 2 readiness: for customers requesting this independent report.
  3. DPDP readiness: Indian personal-data duties, with staged commencement.
  4. GDPR readiness: where the customer’s activities fall within EU rules.
  5. PCI DSS readiness: specialist work for payment-related customers.

For a payments-focused customer base, PCI may come first. ISO 27001 leads to an independent certificate; SOC 2 to an independent accounting firm’s report; privacy laws create ongoing duties. They are not interchangeable badges.
